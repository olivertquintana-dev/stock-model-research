# V3 Portfolio Backtest

Historical research backtest of a monthly rebalanced equal-weight Top 20 RS63 portfolio.

- Eligibility: price >= 5.
- Signal: trailing 63-session return.
- Portfolio: top 20 names, equal weight.
- Rebalance: monthly.
- Forward return: next 21 sessions.
- Cost sensitivity: 10 bps multiplied by a simple constituent turnover proxy.

This is a research backtest, not evidence of executable performance. Point-in-time universe, liquidity, spreads, corporate actions and realistic execution remain limitations.
