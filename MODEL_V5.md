# V5 Portfolio Construction

Compares equal-weight monthly RS63 portfolios of 10, 20, 30, 50 and 100 names.

Common assumptions:
- Price >= 5
- 63-session momentum signal
- Monthly rebalance
- Next 21-session return
- 10 bps multiplied by constituent turnover

Decision objective: identify whether greater diversification improves risk-adjusted performance relative to the concentrated Top 20 implementation.
