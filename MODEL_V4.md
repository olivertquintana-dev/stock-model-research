# V4 Risk & Cost Analysis

Evaluates the V3 monthly Top 20 RS63 portfolio across:
- annualized return and volatility
- zero-risk-free Sharpe proxy
- zero-risk-free Sortino proxy
- maximum drawdown
- best and worst months
- negative-month frequency
- transaction-cost sensitivity at 10, 30 and 50 bps per unit of turnover
- annual return stability

These remain research statistics and do not solve point-in-time universe, liquidity or execution limitations.
